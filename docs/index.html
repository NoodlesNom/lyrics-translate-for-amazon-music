Lyrics Translate & Romanize for Amazon Music is a free Microsoft Edge extension. It translates the lyrics in the Amazon Music web player (music.amazon.com) as they scroll, and adds an easy-to-read romanization under lines written in Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Cyrillic, Arabic, Hindi, Thai, Greek, Hebrew and other non-Latin scripts. And when Amazon has no lyrics for a song, it finds synced lyrics on LRCLIB and shows them in the full view, right where Amazon's own lyrics would be.

Features

Everything happens inside the lyrics view you already use. Open a song's lyrics in the Amazon Music web player and the extension does the rest.

♪
New in 1.3

Lyrics for songs Amazon doesn't have

No lyrics on Amazon? The extension looks the song up on LRCLIB, a free, open lyrics database, and shows the lyrics in Amazon's full view, in the same spot and style as Amazon's own, with the current line highlighted as the song plays. Translated and romanized too.

G

Gemini or Google Translate

Use the free Google Translate web service with no key or account, or paste your own free Gemini API key for natural translations that use the whole song as context. The key is saved only in your browser.

⇄

Automatic fallback

If Gemini hits a quota, rejects the key, times out (20 s) or sends an unexpected reply, that song is translated with Google Translate instead, without interrupting you. Gemini is retried after a short pause.

Aa

Romanization

Non-Latin lines get a romanization in small text: romaji for Japanese, pinyin for Chinese, Revised Romanization for Korean, plus Cyrillic, Arabic, Hebrew, Devanagari, Thai, Greek and more. Latin-script lines never get one.

⤒

Translation as the main line

By default the translation replaces the original as the big, bold line, with the romanization underneath. Turn on Original lyrics in the popup to keep the original big and show the extras in small text.

◉

Floating button

A small round button in the top-right corner of the player opens the popup as a panel right on the page. Click outside it or press Esc to close it, or hide the button in the popup.

⟳

Per-song cache

Results are saved per song, translator and language: up to 2000 songs (400 lines each). Replays are instant and make no requests. When the cache is full, the least recently played songs are removed first.

✓
Improved in 1.3

Smarter language check

Songs already in your language are left alone and never go to Gemini, even if the lyrics contain symbols, emoji or a stray look-alike letter from another alphabet. A mostly English song with a few lines in another language only gets those few lines translated.

↻

"Translate this song"

If a mixed-language song was left partly untranslated, one click translates the whole song again with the selected translator and remembers not to skip it next time.

A+

Text size

Scale all the lyric text the extension shows from 85% to 145% (Small, Default, Large, Extra large) without zooming the rest of the player, so auto-scrolling keeps working.

New in 1.3

Lyrics for songs Amazon doesn't have

Some songs have no lyrics on Amazon Music. When that happens, the extension finds synced lyrics on LRCLIB and shows them in the full view, in the same place and style as Amazon's own lyrics.

  • The current line is highlighted and follows playback, seeks and pauses.
  • Translation and romanization work just like on Amazon's lyrics.
  • The popup's This song line shows the title, artist and where the lyrics came from.
  • Only the song's title, artist and duration are sent to lrclib.net. Turn it off anytime with Find lyrics when Amazon has none.

Read along in any script

Can't read the script? Follow the romanization line as the song plays. Here three invented Japanese lines keep the original as the main line, with romaji and the English translation underneath, at the Large text size.

Original lyrics, when you want them

With Original lyrics on, the original stays the big line. Songs that mix scripts are handled line by line: each line gets its own romanization and translation, and lines already in your language are left alone.

Settings without leaving the player

The floating button opens the full popup as an in-page panel, handy in full-screen or when the toolbar is hidden. It's the same popup, with the same live status lines.

The popup

Click the toolbar icon (or the floating button). Changes apply immediately to the open lyrics.

Translate to
Target language: 23 languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Arabic, Hindi and more.
Translator
Gemini (needs key) or Google Translate. Gemini is picked automatically once you save a key.
Gemini status dot
Gemini working, last reply 2 min ago · Using Google (quota hit, timed out, error or unexpected reply) · Invalid key · No key, not used yet, or Google selected.
This song
The current song's title and artist, its detected language(s) and state, for example "Japanese + English · Translated with Gemini (from cache)", "English · Already in English, no translation needed" or "Translating…". For songs Amazon has no lyrics for, a second line says where the lyrics came from: "Lyrics added from LRCLIB (synced)", or "Amazon has no lyrics; none found on LRCLIB".
Translate this song
Translates the open song again with the selected translator and stops it being skipped.
Text size
Small (85%), Default, Large (120%) or Extra large (145%).
Toggles
Under Show: Romanization, Translation, Original lyrics and Floating button. Below them, Find lyrics when Amazon has none (on by default; turn it off and nothing is ever sent to LRCLIB).
Saved songs
For example "Saved songs: 12 (Gemini 8 · Google 6) · ~0.3 MB": how many songs are cached, how many are fully translated by each translator, and roughly how much storage is used.
Check for updates
Unpacked copies only: a small link at the bottom of the popup that asks GitHub for the latest version and shows "You're up to date (v1.3.5)" or "Update available: vX.Y.Z · Download" with a one-line how-to. They also check on their own about once a day and put a small NEW badge on the icon when an update is out. Store copies don't show it; Edge updates them.
Settings
The link at the top of the popup. Opens the settings page, where you save, test or remove your Gemini key.

Optional: use Gemini with your own free key

  1. Get a free API key at Google AI Studio.
  2. Click the extension icon, then Settings. Paste the key, click Save, then Test key.
  3. In the popup, Translator switches to Gemini (needs key). Romanization still comes from Google Translate.

Free-tier rate limits and daily quotas apply, and on the free tier Google may use the content sent to improve its products. The key is stored only in this browser (not synced) and sent only to Google's Gemini API, in a request header.

Privacy

No data collection

The developer collects nothing. There are no analytics, no trackers, no accounts and no servers of our own.

What is sent, and where

Only the lyric lines on screen are sent to Google Translate, and to Google Gemini if you added your own key, only to translate or romanize them. No cookies, song history or other data.

LRCLIB, only when needed

Only for songs Amazon has no lyrics for, the song's title, artist and duration are sent to lrclib.net to find lyrics. No personal data, no account. Switch it off with Find lyrics when Amazon has none.

Update check (unpacked only)

Copies installed with Load unpacked ask GitHub's public API for the latest version number about once a day and when you press Check for updates. Nothing personal is sent. Store copies never do.

Stored in your browser

Your Gemini key, the translation cache and the LRCLIB lyrics cache stay in local extension storage; your settings are in sync storage. Removing the extension clears them.

Install

From Microsoft Edge Add-ons

The easiest way: install it from the official store with one click, and Edge keeps it updated automatically. Store copies never contact GitHub.

Get it on Microsoft Edge Add-ons

Load unpacked (Chrome and other browsers)

  1. Download the ZIP from the latest release and unzip it (or clone the repo and use its extension folder).
  2. Open edge://extensions (in Chrome: chrome://extensions).
  3. Turn on Developer mode.
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the folder that contains manifest.json.
  5. Open music.amazon.com, play a song and open the lyrics view.

Unpacked copies tell you when a new version is out (a small NEW badge and a download link in the popup) and have a Check for updates link at the bottom of the popup. To update, unzip the new ZIP over your folder and press Reload on the extension card.

Works on music.amazon.com (not regional domains such as .co.jp or .de). The Google Translate web endpoint is unofficial and rate-limited; if it refuses requests, lines stay as they are and the extension retries about a minute later.
